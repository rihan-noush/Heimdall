What we look at

The boring details that catch the expensive mistakes.

  • Domain age

    Freshly registered names are a classic phishing move. We pull WHOIS creation dates and treat missing records as a signal, not a pass.

  • Lookalike hosts

    Character swaps, extra hyphens, and near-brand spellings get flagged before you type a password.

  • Infrastructure

    Cheap hosting, odd TLS, and throwaway nameservers tend to cluster. We score the neighborhood, not just the homepage.

  • Known bad lists

    Open threat feeds and prior reports — when a domain is already burned, you should not have to discover it yourself.
